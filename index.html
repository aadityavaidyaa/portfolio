01 — About

A little about how I work.

I'm Aaditya Vaidya, a 3D artist working across modeling, texturing, and simulation. I like projects where the technical side is as interesting as the visual one — a fluid sim that needs to read as real, a material that needs to hold up under a hero camera angle.

My process usually starts in Maya or Blender for the base form, moves through Substance Painter for surfacing, and often ends in Houdini or Embergen when something needs to move, break, or burn. Final polish happens in Unreal Engine or After Effects depending on where the piece lives.

Outside of client work I spend time on personal simulation studies, which is usually where my best ideas come from.
